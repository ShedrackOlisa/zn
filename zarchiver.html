Tips For Getting The Most Out Of It:
• Use the "Test Archive" option before extracting a large download, it catches corrupted or incomplete files before you waste time on a failed extraction.
• For OBB files, extract directly into the Android/obb folder, ZArchiver has the file permissions needed to write there without extra steps.
• 7z compression shrinks files noticeably more than ZIP if storage space is tight and extraction speed isn't a priority.
• Pin your most-used download folder for quicker access instead of navigating your storage from scratch each time.
• Grant storage permission fully when prompted, partial permissions are the most common reason extraction silently fails.
