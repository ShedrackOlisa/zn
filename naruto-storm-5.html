Bringing Boruto's cast into the Storm engine

The mod's biggest draw is characters the official Storm series never got around to - Boruto in both base and Karma forms, Sarada with her Chidori and Sharingan, Mitsuki's Sage Mode, and antagonists like Kawaki and Jigen with his Isshiki transformation. Watching these newer characters move using Storm 4's animation framework is genuinely impressive fan work, even if a few movesets feel adapted rather than purpose-built.

Team Ultimate combos add real spectacle

Beyond individual awakenings, the mod introduces three-character Team Ultimate attacks - Boruto, Sarada, and Mitsuki chaining a Team 7-style combo finisher is a genuine highlight, and seeing Naruto and Sasuke's Final Valley teamwork recreated as a coordinated attack hits a nostalgic note the base Storm 4 never offered. These sequences are clearly where the mod team spent their effort, and it shows in how polished they feel compared to some of the newer character animations.

The same combat foundation, some new toys

Underneath the new roster, this is still Storm 4's chakra-dash, substitution-jutsu combat system, now with Karma and Isshiki awakening modes layered on top for the newer characters. If you've played Storm 4, the muscle memory transfers directly, and the new mechanics integrate reasonably well rather than feeling bolted on. It's proof the mod team understood the underlying system rather than just reskinning assets.

Being realistic about what a mod is

This needs an honest caveat: it's unofficial, unpolished in places, and balance is nowhere near what Bandai Namco would ship. Some newer characters feel stronger than others, a few animations are clearly adapted rather than built from scratch, and you may run into the occasional bug or missing feature compared to a proper commercial release. Go in with fan-project expectations, not console-launch ones, and it's a lot easier to enjoy for what it is.

Is Naruto Storm 5 (Fan-Made) Worth Downloading?
