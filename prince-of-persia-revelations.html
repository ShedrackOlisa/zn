Dahaka Chases Are Still The Series' Best Panic Button Moments

Nothing in the Prince of Persia trilogy matches the pure adrenaline of a Dahaka chase - the moment that creature starts hunting you, the game turns into a sprint through half-remembered platforming routes with zero room for error. Revelations keeps these sequences exactly as tense as they were on console, and the added areas give you a couple of fresh routes to panic through, which honestly makes replaying the chases more interesting than they had any right to be.

Dual-Wield Combat Rewards Aggression Over Patience

Unlike Sands of Time's more measured swordplay, combat here wants you to be reckless. Pick up a secondary weapon off a fallen enemy and start chaining combos between your main blade and whatever you've scavenged - maces, axes, daggers all change your combo timing in small but noticeable ways. The new stealth-kill options added for this PSP release give you a quieter way through some of the tougher enemy clusters, which is a welcome addition since Warrior Within's difficulty spikes could get brutal on the original release.

New Areas Add Real Verticality To Familiar Spaces

The expanded content isn't just padding - the new rooms and passages are built with the same wall-running, pole-swinging logic as the rest of the game, and a few of them genuinely rank among the best platforming puzzles in the series. If you've run through Warrior Within before, these sections are the actual reason to revisit Revelations rather than just replaying the original.

Tips Before You Jump In:
• Keep a secondary weapon on hand at all times - dual-wield combos deal noticeably more damage than the sword alone.
• During Dahaka chases, don't stop to fight anything - the moment you're being hunted, every second of hesitation counts against you.
• Use the new stealth-kill routes when you can; they save health and time compared to fighting groups head-on.
• Set "Buffered Rendering" on in PPSSPP for smoother wall-run animations during the tighter platforming sections.

Performance On PPSSPP

Revelations runs cleanly on the emulator, holding a steady frame rate through combat and platforming alike. The new areas don't introduce any noticeable slowdown, and the compressed ISO trims the file size without visibly hurting the game's already-moody, shadow-heavy art direction. It's an easy game to recommend for older or budget Android devices.

Is Prince Of Persia Revelations Worth Your Time?

If you've already beaten Warrior Within on console, the new areas and stealth options make this worth a revisit, especially for series completionists. If you haven't played Warrior Within at all, this is actually a fine way to experience it - you get the darker tone, the tense Dahaka chases, and some bonus content the original release never had. Just go in expecting a tougher, meaner game than Sands of Time.
