Why FIFA 16 Was the Peak of Mobile Football

I'll stand by this claim: FIFA 16 on mobile is the best pure football game EA ever put on a phone. Touch controls were refined without being oversimplified, ball physics let you take heavy touches and misplace passes in ways that actually reflected player skill differences, and pace mattered but never at the expense of strength and positioning. Career mode had genuine substance too, you could take a struggling club and build it into champions over several seasons through real decisions rather than currency grinding. Later entries added visual polish but lost some of that substance, which is exactly why this specific engine keeps getting revived by modders.
