Chain Wrestling And Limb Targeting: A Slower, Smarter Match

What separates this from the arcade-style SmackDown vs Raw era is the pace. Matches build through chain grappling sequences where whoever wins the initial lock-up gets the advantage, and targeting a specific limb early pays dividends later when you go for a submission. Stamina genuinely matters too, spam moves without pacing yourself and you'll gas out at the worst possible moment. It's a more patient, strategic style of wrestling game, and it rewards players who treat a match like an actual contest rather than a race to the finisher.

MyRise: Building A Career From The Ground Up

MyRise lets you create your own wrestler and choose your path, starting in indie bingo halls and working toward WrestleMania main events. The branching storylines respond to your alignment, playing as a fan-favorite babyface or a hated heel changes how other wrestlers and storylines react to you, giving repeat playthroughs genuinely different flavor rather than just cosmetic variation.

Universe Mode: Total Control Over Your Promotion

If MyRise is about one wrestler's story, Universe mode is about running the whole show. You book cards across Raw, SmackDown, and NXT, manufacture rivalries, and can either simulate results automatically or manually control every match yourself. It's a genuinely deep booking sandbox that can eat entire evenings once you start engineering your own long-term storylines.

Tips Before Your First Match:
• Learn reversal timing in practice mode first, the window here is tighter than the older arcade-style entries.
• Target limbs early if you're planning a submission finish, the payoff compounds the longer you commit to it.
• In MyRise, your face/heel choices shape your storyline options, pick a direction and commit rather than flip-flopping.
• Universe mode can run itself on auto-simulate if you'd rather just watch your promotion evolve, don't feel obligated to manually book every match.
• If performance dips during multi-man matches, lower rendering resolution before touching gameplay settings.

Is WWE 2K23 Worth Downloading?
