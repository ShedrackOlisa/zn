The Bound System Adds Real Depth To Every Combo

Landing a specific move on an airborne opponent bounces them off the ground, opening a window to extend your combo into launch-bound-juggle-wall carry sequences that can strip 50% of a health bar from a single opening. It's a genuinely creative addition to the combo game, rewarding players who put in the time to learn the routes, and even with the PSP's slightly different timing window compared to console, it remains satisfying to pull off.

Scenario Campaign Is A Weird, Genuinely Fun Detour

Rather than another arcade ladder, the Scenario Campaign drops you into a beat-'em-up with light RPG progression - leveling up your character, equipping gear, and following a story about Lars and Alisa searching for Jin. It plays almost like a different game entirely, and at 5-10 hours long depending on how many side missions you tackle, it gives the package a legitimate single-player mode beyond just fighting the CPU repeatedly.

The Rage System Keeps Close Matches Tense Until The End

When your health drops low, your character enters Rage - a visual and mechanical shift that boosts your damage output and gives you a genuine chance at a comeback. It's a smart tension-building mechanic that keeps close matches exciting right up to the final hit, and it's one of the additions from this era of Tekken that stuck around in later entries for good reason.

Tips Before You Jump In:
• Learn a basic Bound combo route early - even a simple one adds significant damage over just launching and juggling.
• Lars is a great beginner pick thanks to his straightforward, high-damage moveset.
• Use the Scenario Campaign to practice movement and combos in a lower-pressure setting before jumping into versus matches.
• Watch your health bar for Rage activation - both yours and your opponent's, since a losing fighter can turn a match around fast.
• A Bluetooth controller makes a big difference for precise Bound combo timing compared to touch controls.
