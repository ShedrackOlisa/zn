VS Attack Is the Best Thing EA Added This Year

If you've never played VS Attack, it's a turn-based twist on head-to-head football, you and your opponent alternate short possessions, each trying to score or defend within a limited window, rather than playing a full 90 minutes back and forth in real time. It sounds gimmicky on paper, but it's genuinely well-suited to mobile sessions, a match takes five minutes instead of fifteen, and the turn structure means lag and connection hiccups barely matter. It's become my go-to mode when I only have a short break, and it rewards tactical substitutions and formation tweaks more than raw mechanical skill.

Career Mode's Youth System Actually Has Teeth

EA finally gave Career Mode a youth academy worth engaging with. You scout regional prospects, assign them training focus, and watch their attributes shift over a season instead of getting a flat stat boost. I picked up a 66-rated left-back through my academy, and by the end of a single season of rotation minutes he'd developed into a legitimate first-team option. Transfers around him also feel less arbitrary now, AI clubs make offers that roughly track a player's form and age rather than firing random bids at your best players every transfer window, which was a constant annoyance in older FIFA Mobile builds.

Licensed Clubs and Presentation Upgrades

The licensing here covers most major European leagues along with a solid chunk of South American and Asian clubs, and the presentation package finally looks console-adjacent, real stadium likenesses, broadcast-style camera cuts on goals, and commentary that reacts to context instead of looping generic lines. It's not going to fool anyone into thinking they're on a console, but it's a real step up from the flatter presentation FIFA Mobile shipped with for years.

Performance on Mid-Range Phones

I ran this on a mid-range device with no dedicated gaming chipset, and it held up better than expected. Without full crowd rendering or the densest pitch detail turned on, frame rates stayed stable through full matches, VS Attack in particular never stuttered. The APK plus OBB combo does need close to 2GB of storage, so make sure you've got room before downloading, and a stable connection matters since most modes lean on EA's servers even with the mod's unlocks applied.
