It's a smart way to keep the mode engaging once the usual free-to-play friction is removed.

Performance and Storage Reality Check

This is a demanding install. My phone did warm up during longer sessions with graphics maxed, so if you're on a mid-range device, dropping a setting or two keeps things comfortable without sacrificing much visual quality. Just make sure you clear enough storage before downloading, this isn't a game you can squeeze onto an already-full phone.
