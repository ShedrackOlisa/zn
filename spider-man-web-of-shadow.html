Two Suits, Two Genuinely Different Ways To Play

This isn't a cosmetic choice - the black suit turns Spider-Man into a slower, heavier brawler who slams enemies into the pavement and chains brutal ground combos, while the red suit keeps things acrobatic and mobile, favoring aerial combat and quick repositioning. Switching mid-fight to match the situation in front of you is genuinely satisfying, and it gives the combat far more depth than most licensed superhero games ever bother with.

A City That Reacts To The Symbiote Outbreak Around You

New York doesn't just sit there as a backdrop - symbiotes grab civilians in the street in front of you, forcing you to make split-second choices about whether to dive in and save them or push forward toward the main objective. It creates a sense of a world under active siege rather than a static open world waiting for you to interact with it, and that dynamic tension carries through the whole story.

Combat Rewards Chaining Aerial And Ground Attacks Together

The combo system encourages juggling enemies between air and ground states, building toward genuinely long combo strings once you understand the timing. Pulling off an extended aerial combo against a symbiote-corrupted enemy feels like a real skill expression rather than button-mashing luck, and the PSP port's combat responsiveness holds up well against the console originals.
