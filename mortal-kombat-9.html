X-Ray Moves And Fatalities: Still Ruthless

The X-Ray super attacks are the headline addition, slow-motion moments where you watch bones crack and ribs cave in from the inside. Fatalities remain gloriously creative and gruesome - Scorpion's "Toasty," Sub-Zero's spine rip, and the rest of the roster's finishers are all present. Story Mode: The Best Retelling The Series Has Had

The thing that makes this version so impressive is that it keeps everything that made the console version great. The story mode retells the events of MK1 through MK3 across multiple playable characters, with cutscenes that are genuinely well produced for a fighting game. It's not going to win any writing awards, but it's engaging, it respects long-time fans, and at four to six hours long it gives you a real reason to stick around beyond the arcade ladder.

Tips Before You Step Into The Kombat Pit:

• A physical controller makes a real difference for fatality inputs - touch controls make the tight timing much harder.
